Shipping over polishing

The plan written on day one comes from the day you understood the problem least. Every week you wait, you know more.

A decision you call permanent is expensive to change, because changing it means admitting you were wrong. Call everything temporary and it costs nothing to change. Then you change things when you should.

The price is that I can't promise anyone a sequence.
